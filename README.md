# 🔭 Macroscope

### **A clearer lens on economic health.**

Macroscope turns a sea of economic numbers into something you can actually *read*.

Instead of staring at isolated GDP, inflation, employment, trade, and financial indicators, Macroscope connects them into a **multidimensional picture of how an economy is performing.**

**Raw data → meaningful signals → economic dimensions → one bigger picture.**

---

## 🌍 Look Beyond the Numbers

An economy is never just *one* number.

Macroscope brings together indicators across:

`Labor` · `Consumption` · `Prices` · `Finance` · `Production` · `Growth` · `Trade` · `Markets`

Each signal is interpreted according to its economic meaning, normalized onto a common scale, and brought into a broader analytical framework.

The result?

### **An Economy Health Score**

A single, intuitive signal that gives you the *big picture* — without hiding the details behind it.

---

## 🧠 How It Thinks

Macroscope works in layers:

```text
Economic Data
      ↓
Indicator Intelligence
      ↓
Normalized Signals
      ↓
Economic Dimensions
      ↓
Economy Health
```

So instead of asking:

> *“What is the inflation rate?”*

you can start asking:

> *“What is inflation telling us about the economy?”*

That shift—from **numbers to meaning**—is the heart of Macroscope.

---

## 📈 See the Economy Move

Economic health isn't static.

Macroscope brings historical perspective into the picture, making it easier to spot:

**Momentum ↗ · Pressure ↘ · Stability → · Change**

You can move from the overall health of an economy down into the categories and individual indicators shaping that picture.

---

**Next.js** · **React** · **TypeScript**
**Tailwind CSS** · **Recharts** · **Lucide React**

A modern frontend, structured economic data, custom analytical logic, and visualization working together as one system.

---

## 🎯 The Goal

Macroscope isn't trying to give you *more* economic data.

There is already plenty of that.

It's trying to make the data **make sense**.

> **Less noise. More context. Better perspective.**
